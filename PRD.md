# PRD: Post 008 — Naive Bayes & LDA

**AI Engineering Lab Series | Era 1: Classic Machine Learning**

## Objective
Teach Gaussian Naive Bayes and LDA using two multi-class classification problems.

## Datasets
| Dataset | Domain | Rows | Classes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jira Bug Ticket Routing | General (DevOps) | 8,000 | 6 |
| Silicon Defect Classification | Post-Silicon Validation | 6,000 | 6 |

## Concepts Covered
Bayes' theorem, class-conditional distributions, prior/likelihood/posterior, Gaussian NB, Multinomial NB, LDA vs PCA, decision boundaries, confusion matrix, multi-class metrics.
